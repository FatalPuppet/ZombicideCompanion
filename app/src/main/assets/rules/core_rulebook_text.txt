MOVEMENT
  Movement is life. Keep on moving!
                                                         - Wanda

Actors can move from one Zone to the next as long as the
first Zone shares at least one edge with the destination
Zone. Corners do not count. This means Actors cannot move
diagonally.

In street Zones, movement from one empty Zone to another
has no restrictions. However, Actors must go through an
open door (or opening) to move from a building Zone to a
street Zone and vice-versa.

In building Zones, Actors may move from one Zone to
another as long as their Zones are linked by an opening (such
as an open door). The position of a miniature in the Zone and
the layout of the walls do not matter as long as the Zones
share an opening.
